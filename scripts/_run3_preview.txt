=== tier1a_paye_018_20260603 ===

EN: Yes. Director's fees are subject to PAYE. If the director is not a full-time employee, the fees may be subject to withholding tax instead of PAYE — the rate is 15% for residents. Confirm the director's classification with TRA.

=== tier1a_paye_extended_004_20260603 ===
SW: Mwajiri lazima aandae na kuwasilisha Fomu P9 kwa kila mfanyakazi ifikapo tarehe 31 Januari ya mwaka unaofuata mwaka wa mapato. Fomu P9 inaonyesha jumla ya mshahara wa mwaka, PAYE iliyokatwa, na mchango wa NSSF. Nakala moja inatolewa kwa mfanyakazi na moja inawasilishwa TRA. Kuchelewa kuwasilisha fomu P9 kunasababisha adhabu.
EN: An employer must prepare and submit Form P9 for each employee by 31 January of the year following the tax year. Form P9 shows the total annual salary, PAYE deducted, and NSSF contributions. One copy is given to the employee and one is filed with TRA. Late submission of Form P9 attracts penalties.

=== tier1a_paye_extended_018_20260603 ===
SW: Mwajiri anatoa Fomu P45 (au hati sawa) ikionyesha jumla ya mshahara na PAYE iliyokatwa hadi tarehe ya kumaliza kazi. Fomu hii inasaidia mwajiri mpya kuhesabu PAYE kwa usahihi kwa sehemu ya mwaka iliyobaki. Pia, ifikapo Januari 31 ya mwaka unaofuata, mwajiri anatoa Fomu P9 ikionyesha mapato ya mwaka mzima na PAYE yote iliyokatwa. Mfanyakazi anahifadhi fomu hizi kwa matumizi ya return ya kodi ya mtu binafsi.
EN: The employer issues Form P45 (or equivalent) showing total earnings and PAYE deducted up to the date of leaving. This form helps the new employer calculate PAYE correctly for the remainder of the year. Also, by 31 January of the following year, the employer issues Form P9 showing full-year earnings and total PAYE deducted. The employee retains these forms for their individual tax return.

=== tier1a_income_tax_007_20260603 ===
SW: Ndiyo. Hasara ya biashara inayothibitishwa na TRA inaweza kubebwa mbele na kutumiwa kupunguza mapato yanayotozwa kodi ya miaka ijayo. Hata hivyo, kuna kikomo cha muda: hasara inaweza kubebwa mbele kwa kipindi fulani tu kulingana na Sheria ya Kodi ya Mapato — thibitisha muda halisi wa sasa na TRA au mshauri wa kodi kwa sababu kanuni hii inapitiwa mara kwa mara. Hasara haiwezi kubebwa nyuma (carry back) nchini Tanzania.
EN: Yes. A business loss confirmed by TRA can be carried forward and used to reduce taxable income in future years. However, there is a time limit: losses can only be carried forward for a specific period under the Income Tax Act — confirm the current period with TRA or a tax consultant as this rule is reviewed periodically. Losses cannot be carried back in Tanzania.

=== tier1a_biz_lic_004_20260603 ===
SW: Ndiyo. Mgahawa na biashara za chakula zinahitaji leseni maalum za ziada, ambazo ni pamoja na: leseni ya chakula (food handling certificate) kutoka kwa Mamlaka ya Chakula na Dawa Tanzania (TFDA/TMDA), cheti cha afya (health certificate) cha watumishi wote wanaoshughulikia chakula, ukaguzi wa mazingira na usafi wa mazingira (environmental health inspection) kutoka Halmashauri, na wakati mwingine leseni ya biashara ya vinywaji. Ukaguzi wa mara kwa mara hufanywa na maafisa wa afya wa Halmashauri.
EN: Yes. Restaurants and food businesses require additional special licences beyond the standard LGA licence, including: a food handling certificate from the Tanzania Food and Drugs Authority (TFDA/TMDA), health certificates for all food handlers, an environmental and public health inspection from the Council, and sometimes a beverages business licence. Regular inspections are carried out by council health officers.

=== tier1a_biz_lic_009_20260603 ===
SW: Kliniki ya kibinafsi na duka la dawa vinahitaji: idhini ya kufungua (facility licence) kutoka Wizara ya Afya au Bodi ya Madawa Tanzania (Medical Council of Tanganyika), usajili wa daktari/mfamasia kutoka Baraza la Madawa (MCT) au Baraza la Wauguzi, leseni ya TMDA kwa biashara za dawa, leseni ya LGA ya halmashauri husika, na usajili wa BRELA. Ukaguzi wa TMDA hufanywa kabla ya kufungua na mara kwa mara baada ya kufungua. Kufungua bila idhini ni kosa zito la jinai.
EN: A private clinic and pharmacy require: a facility operating licence from the Ministry of Health or Medical Council of Tanganyika (MCT), individual registration of the doctor/pharmacist from MCT or Nursing Council, a TMDA licence for pharmaceutical businesses, an LGA licence from the relevant council, and BRELA registration. TMDA inspection is conducted before opening and periodically thereafter. Opening without authorisation is a serious criminal offence.

=== tier1a_brela_chg_001_20260603 ===
SW: Kubadilisha mkurugenzi wa kampuni kunahitaji: uamuzi wa bodi (board resolution) ukimteua mkurugenzi mpya na kumalizia mamlaka ya mkurugenzi wa zamani, kuwasilisha Fomu 23 (Taarifa ya Mabadiliko ya Wakurugenzi) kwa BRELA ndani ya siku 14 baada ya mabadiliko, kulipa ada inayohusiana, na kuwasilisha hati za utambulisho za mkurugenzi mpya. BRELA itasasisha rekodi na kutoa uthibitisho. Kuchelewa kuwasilisha mabadiliko kunasababisha adhabu.
EN: Changing a company director requires: a board resolution appointing the new director and ending the authority of the outgoing director, filing Form 23 (Notice of Change of Directors) with BRELA within 14 days of the change, paying the associated fee, and submitting identification documents of the new director. BRELA will update the records and issue confirmation. Late filing of changes attracts penalties.

=== tier1a_brela_chg_004_20260603 ===
SW: Ndiyo. Uhamishaji wa hisa kati ya wanahisa lazima uripotiwe BRELA. Mchakato ni: kuandaa hati ya uhamishaji wa hisa (instrument of transfer), kupiga stempu hati hiyo (kodi ya stempu ya 1% — ona Stamp Duty Act), kuwasilisha taarifa ya mabadiliko ya wanahisa kwa BRELA, na kusasisha daftari la wanahisa (register of members) la kampuni. BRELA itasasisha rekodi na kutoa uthibitisho wa mabadiliko ya umiliki. Bila taarifa ya BRELA, uhamishaji hauna nguvu ya kisheria.
EN: Yes. Transfer of shares between shareholders must be reported to BRELA. The process is: prepare an instrument of transfer, stamp the instrument (1% stamp duty — see Stamp Duty Act), file a shareholder change notification with BRELA, and update the company's register of members. BRELA will update records and issue confirmation of the ownership change. Without BRELA notification, the transfer has no legal force.

=== tier1a_compliance_002_20260603 ===
SW: PRN (Payment Reference Number) ni nambari ya kipekee inayotolewa na mfumo wa TRA baada ya kuwasilisha return au kutoa ombi la malipo. PRN inathibitisha kwamba malipo yanakwenda kwenye akaunti sahihi ya kodi sahihi. Unapata PRN kwa: kuwasilisha return yako kwenye IDRAS au portal ya TRA, ambapo PRN itatolewa moja kwa moja baada ya kuthibitisha return. Kisha unalipa kupitia GePG kwa kutumia PRN hiyo. Kila PRN ina muda wa kuisha (kawaida siku chache) — lipa kabla haijakwisha. Bila PRN sahihi, malipo yanaweza kutokwenda kwenye akaunti yako ya kodi.
EN: A PRN (Payment Reference Number) is a unique number issued by the TRA system after filing a return or making a payment request. The PRN confirms that payment is going to the correct tax account. You obtain a PRN by: filing your return on IDRAS or the TRA portal, where a PRN is generated automatically after confirming the return. You then pay via GePG using that PRN. Each PRN has an expiry period (usually a few days) — pay before it expires. Without the correct PRN, payment may not reach your tax account.

=== tier1a_compliance_004_20260603 ===
SW: Muda wa kupata cheti cha usafi wa kodi unategemea hali ya akaunti yako ya kodi na mzigo wa kazi wa TRA. Kwa kawaida: ikiwa akaunti yako ya kodi iko safi (hakuna madeni, return zote zimefungwa, malipo yote yalithibitishwa), cheti kinaweza kutolewa ndani ya siku 3–7 za kazi. Ikiwa kuna madeni yanayosubiri au return ambazo hazikuwasilishwa, utahitajika kutatua matatizo hayo kwanza. Ombi linafanywa kwenye portal ya TRA au kwa maombi ya maandishi kwenye ofisi ya TRA. Epuka kuomba siku chache kabla ya tarehe ya mwisho ya tender.
EN: The time to obtain a tax clearance certificate depends on the status of your tax account and TRA's workload. Generally: if your tax account is clean (no debts, all returns filed, all payments confirmed), the certificate may be issued within 3–7 working days. If there are outstanding debts or unfiled returns, you must resolve those issues first. The application is made on TRA's portal or in writing at a TRA office. Avoid applying a few days before a tender deadline.

=== tier1a_compliance_009_20260603 ===
SW: Walipa kodi wa mara ya kwanza (first-time offenders) wanaowasiliana na TRA kwa hiari kabla ya hatua za utekelezaji na ambao wana rekodi nzuri ya utii wa kodi wana uwezekano mkubwa wa kupata samahani au upunguzaji wa adhabu. Kwa kesi ya PAYE iliyochelewa mara moja: lipa PAYE yote ya msingi pamoja na riba ya kuchelewa, kisha omba rasmi samahani ya adhabu ukitaja historia yako nzuri ya utii. TRA inazingatia kila kesi kwa mtu binafsi. Samahani si ya lazima kisheria — ni uamuzi wa Kamishna. Kujitokeza mapema na kwa hiari ni sababu kubwa ya mafanikio ya ombi.
EN: First-time offenders who proactively approach TRA before enforcement steps and who have a good compliance history have a higher chance of receiving a waiver or reduction of penalties. For a single late PAYE case: pay all principal PAYE plus late payment interest, then formally apply for a penalty waiver citing your good compliance history. TRA considers each case individually. A waiver is not a legal right — it is the Commissioner's discretionary decision. Coming forward early and voluntarily is a major factor in a successful application.
