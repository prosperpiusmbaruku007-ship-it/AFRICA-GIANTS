## Item 2 — Medicines and approved medical equipment: VAT-EXEMPT, not zero-rated

**Source (VAT Act Cap.148 R.E.2023, Schedule made under s.6(1), PART I "SUPPLIES AND IMPORTS EXEMPT FROM VALUE ADDED TAX", item 7), verbatim:**
> "7. Medicine or pharmaceutical products. 1. Essential Human and veterinary medicine, drugs, medical equipment and packaging material which have been approved by the responsible Minister. Provided that, the packaging material is specifically designed for packing pharmaceutical products."

It is in the same Part I list as the health-care item (item 10). **The Act also says (s.5(3)): "Where a supply is both exempt and zero-rated, the supply shall be zero-rated"**, and zero-rates, among others, exports (s.55(1): goods "exported within the meaning of the term 'export' as provided for under section 2"), immovable property outside the United Republic (s.54) and duty-free supplies to tourists (s.55(2)). **Input tax:** s.72(1)(a) allows a credit where the goods or services were acquired "for the purpose of making taxable supplies"; s.74 gives a formula where only partly. Finance Acts 2024/25/26: item 7 is not named in any (FA2025 s.135 amends Part I items 1, 2, 3, 9, 13, 15, 17, 21, 27, 31, 34; FA2026 s.96 items 3, 4, 6, 27, 31(b)).
**Proposed fact `medicines_vat_exempt`:** "Dawa muhimu za binadamu na za mifugo, dawa (drugs), vifaa tiba na vifungashio vyake VILIVYOIDHINISHWA na Waziri husika ni MSAMAHA wa VAT, si zero-rated (Sheria ya VAT). Si kila dawa au kifaa: kinahitaji idhini ya Waziri; vifungashio vinahusika tu vikiwa vimeundwa mahsusi kufungia bidhaa za dawa. Msamaha maana yake VAT haitozwi kwenye mauzo hayo; mkopo wa input VAT unatolewa kwa manunuzi yaliyofanywa kwa ajili ya kutoa bidhaa au huduma zinazotozwa VAT (taxable supplies), na kwa matumizi ya sehemu hugawanywa kwa fomula. Zero-rated (0%) ni aina nyingine, kama mauzo ya nje; bidhaa ikiwa na msamaha na zero-rating kwa pamoja inakuwa zero-rated (kwa mfano dawa zinazouzwa nje ya nchi). Thibitisha na TRA."
