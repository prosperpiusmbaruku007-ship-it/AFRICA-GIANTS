GOVERNMENT NOTICE No. 605A published on 13/10/2025  LABOUR INSTITUTIONS ACT, (CAP. 300) ________  ORDER ________  (Made under section 39(1))  THE LABOUR INSTITUTIONS (MINIMUM WAGE FOR PRIVATE SECTOR) ORDER, 2025  Citation and commencement   1. This Order may be cited as the Labour Institutions (Minimum Wage for Private Sector) Order, 2025 and shall come into force on the 1st day of January, 2026.   Application  2. This Order shall apply to all employees and employers in the private sector.   Interpretation  3. In this Order, unless the context requires otherwise-       “domestic work” means work performed in or for a household under employment relationship or employment agreement;  “agriculture” includes crop production, forestry activities, fishing, animal husbandry, primary processing, storage, operation or transportation in agricultural undertaking, by or on behalf of the operator of that undertaking, including cooperative societies which are related to agricultural production;  “collective bargaining agreement” means a written agreement concluded by a registered trade union

Sectors and areas  4.-(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order, minimum wage shall be paid in respect of sectors and areas prescribed in First Schedule.  Cap. 366  (2) The calculation of comparable salary rates shall be determined as prescribed in the First Schedule to the Employment and Labour Relations Act.   (3) The minimum wage rates specified in the Second Schedule shall be regarded as the minimum wage payable to employee in the respective sector or area, and an employer may pay such employee an amount above the minimum wage prescribed in respective sector or area.   (4) More favourable terms on the minimum wages rates shall be improved through employment contracts or collective bargaining agreements.   Employment standards   Cap. 366  5.-(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order, employment standards applicable to all employees in each specified sector or area shall be as specified under Part III of the Employment and Labour Relations Act, or as stipulated in employment contracts or collective bargaining agreements.   (2) An employee shall in addition to annual paid leave, be entitled to a leave travel allowance once in every two years of continuous service with the same employer.

  (3) An employee shall be entitled to a subsistence allowance when required by his employer to work outside his work station according to the agreement entered upon between employer and employee.   (4) Without prejudice to the generality of this paragraph, an employee who is a truck driver shall be entitled to an allowance to cover mileage, longer stay outside the work station, loading and unloading of cargo according to negotiations entered between drivers and employers.   Persons enjoying better terms  6. Where any employee to whom the provisions of this Order applies is, at the commencement of this Order, entitled to receive wages at a higher rate than those prescribed or provided for under this Order, that employee shall for so long as is employed by the same employer, continue to receive such higher wages and favourable terms.   Revocation  GN. No. 687 of 2022  7. The Labour Institutions Minimum Wage Order, 2022 is hereby revoked.
